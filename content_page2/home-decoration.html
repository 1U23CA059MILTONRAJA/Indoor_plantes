A room with character

Let your home grow a little greener

Plants add color, shape, and a lived-in feeling to a room. A thoughtful placement is all it takes to make a corner feel complete.

Bring your space to life

Style with leaves and light

Use a taller plant to anchor an empty corner, a trailing plant to soften a shelf, or a small pot to add a quiet touch of green to a desk. Varying leaf shapes and pot textures gives a room natural visual interest.

Before choosing a spot, check the light there and make sure the plant will be easy to reach for watering and care.
