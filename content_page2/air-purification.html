Plants & indoor air

Indoor plants bring living color to a room. Here is what they can—and cannot—do for the air in your home.

A grounded perspective

A natural touch, not an air filter

Some houseplants can absorb certain compounds in laboratory settings. In a typical home, however, a few potted plants are not a substitute for fresh-air ventilation or a properly sized air purifier.

The everyday benefit is simpler: plants add natural color and texture, and tending them can make your space feel cared for and welcoming.
